Senior Analytics Platform Engineer at Healthcare Technology Company

**Job Title: Senior Analytics Platform Engineer**

## Who You'll Work With
As an innovative healthcare technology company, our engineering teams are revolutionizing patient care through advanced data analytics and machine learning solutions. We are passionate about building platforms that enable healthcare providers to make better decisions, improve patient outcomes, and reduce costs. Our collaborative environment brings together engineers, data scientists, and healthcare experts to tackle some of the most important challenges in modern medicine.

We have been recognized for our leadership in healthcare analytics and are seeking a senior platform engineer to join our data infrastructure team. In this impactful role, you will design and build the analytics platforms that power our clinical decision support, population health, and research applications. You will apply your expertise in data engineering, cloud architecture, and healthcare informatics to create scalable, compliant solutions that directly improve patient care.

## What You'll Do
• Architect and implement analytics platform strategy to support our clinical and research applications, including technology roadmap development, infrastructure planning, and cross-team collaboration.
• Lead engineering initiatives through technical leadership, system design, and quality assurance to ensure reliable, scalable platform delivery that meets healthcare compliance requirements.
• Build advanced analytics infrastructure, including real-time data processing, clinical data warehouses, and machine learning platforms that support evidence-based healthcare applications.
• Partner with clinical teams, data scientists, and product managers to understand healthcare workflows and technical requirements, identifying opportunities to improve patient care through better data utilization.
• Implement production-grade healthcare data platforms and cloud infrastructure following HIPAA compliance requirements and healthcare industry best practices.
• Collaborate with our platform engineering teams and clinical informatics specialists to establish standards, share domain expertise, and ensure consistency across our technology ecosystem.
• Drive evaluation of healthcare-specific technologies and integration approaches, including clinical data standards and interoperability frameworks.
• Contribute to our engineering excellence through technical mentoring, architecture reviews, and participation in clinical advisory processes.
• Develop healthcare-specific platform components, monitoring systems, and data quality frameworks optimized for clinical workflows.

## What You'll Bring
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Biomedical Engineering, Health Informatics, or related technical field.
• Strong technical background with 5+ years in data platforms, cloud systems, and software engineering, with at least 3 years in healthcare technology or regulated industries.
• Minimum 3+ years in technical leadership or senior engineering roles, with experience guiding technical decisions and mentoring engineering teams.
• Proficiency in SQL, data pipeline development, cloud platforms, and familiarity with healthcare data standards (HL7, FHIR) and compliance frameworks.
• Experience building production systems in regulated environments with requirements for data privacy, security, and compliance monitoring.
• Demonstrated ability to lead technical teams and foster innovation while maintaining focus on patient safety and regulatory compliance.
• Strong communication skills with ability to work effectively with clinical stakeholders and translate healthcare requirements into technical solutions.

## About Us
We are a mission-driven healthcare technology company focused on improving patient outcomes through innovative data solutions. Our platform serves healthcare providers, researchers, and public health organizations worldwide. We maintain the highest standards for data security and patient privacy while fostering a culture of innovation and clinical excellence. Our diverse team combines deep healthcare expertise with cutting-edge technology capabilities.
