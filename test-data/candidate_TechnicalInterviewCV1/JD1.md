
Cloud Solutions Architect at Tech Consulting Firm

**Job Title: Senior Cloud Solutions Architect**

## Who You'll Work With
As a leading technology consulting firm, our specialists are at the forefront of digital transformation, helping clients realize innovative possibilities. We are passionate about developing strategic solutions and cutting-edge products that address our clients' most challenging business requirements. Our team thrives in an environment filled with complex challenges, forward-thinking professionals, and cutting-edge technologies.

Our organization has been recognized as a premier cloud platform partner multiple times. Leveraging our strong technology partnerships, we are seeking an experienced solutions architect to join our growing team. In this key role, you will lead the design and implementation of cloud-based data platform engagements for our enterprise clients. You will apply your extensive knowledge in data architecture, cloud technologies, and system integration to guide cross-functional teams in delivering robust, scalable solutions that align with business goals.

## What You'll Do
• Design and implement cloud data platform strategies to expand our practice, including identifying growth opportunities, fostering relationships with business leaders, and supporting sales initiatives.
• Guide project teams through technical leadership, mentoring, and quality assurance to ensure successful delivery and client satisfaction.
• Provide expert consulting services, including facilitating technical workshops, architecting solutions, and deploying cloud-native analytics and machine learning platforms.
• Build comprehensive understanding of client business requirements and technical challenges, identifying opportunities to leverage modern data technologies for business transformation.
• Execute hands-on implementation of cloud data platforms and supporting infrastructure following industry best practices for automation and continuous integration.
• Partner with global practice leaders and regional teams to share knowledge, apply expertise, and maintain consistency across our service delivery.
• Drive new business development through proposal responses, solution design, and client presentations.
• Advance our practice capabilities through thought leadership, service innovation, and market expansion initiatives.
• Create and refine reusable frameworks and accelerators for cloud data platforms.

## What You'll Bring
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Systems, or related technical field.
• Strong background with 5+ years in data architecture, cloud platforms, and systems integration, with at least 3 years specializing in modern data platforms.
• Minimum 3+ years in client-facing consulting, with proven ability to understand business needs, design custom solutions, and manage stakeholder relationships.
• Expertise in SQL, data architecture, data pipeline development, and experience with major cloud providers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
• Production experience implementing cloud data platforms using infrastructure automation and DevOps methodologies.
• Proven leadership capabilities in managing and developing technical teams while promoting collaboration and innovation.
• Strong presentation and communication abilities, capable of explaining complex technical concepts to diverse audiences and engaging directly with executive stakeholders.
