Lead Data Platform Engineer at Financial Services Company

**Job Title: Lead Data Platform Engineer**

## Who You'll Work With
As a forward-thinking financial services organization, our technology teams are transforming how we serve clients through innovative data solutions. We focus on building sophisticated platforms and analytical capabilities that solve complex business challenges while maintaining the highest standards of security and compliance. Our engineers work with cutting-edge technologies in a collaborative environment that values both technical excellence and business impact.

We have established ourselves as a leader in cloud-native data architecture and are seeking an experienced platform engineer to join our data engineering organization. In this critical role, you will architect and implement enterprise data platforms that power our analytics, risk management, and customer experience initiatives. You will leverage your deep expertise in distributed systems, cloud architecture, and data engineering to lead technical teams in delivering scalable, secure solutions that drive business value.

## What You'll Do
• Develop and execute data platform strategy to advance our analytics capabilities, including evaluating new technologies, establishing architectural standards, and driving platform adoption across business units.
• Lead engineering teams through technical guidance, code reviews, and architectural oversight to ensure high-quality platform delivery and operational excellence.
• Design and implement enterprise data solutions, including data lakes, streaming platforms, and analytical systems that support real-time and batch processing requirements.
• Collaborate closely with business stakeholders to understand requirements and technical constraints, identifying opportunities to leverage modern data technologies for competitive advantage.
• Build and maintain production data platforms and supporting cloud infrastructure using automated deployment pipelines and site reliability engineering practices.
• Work with global engineering teams and platform architects to establish best practices, share technical knowledge, and ensure consistency across our technology stack.
• Drive technology evaluation and vendor selection processes, including proof-of-concept development and technical assessments.
• Contribute to our engineering culture through technical mentoring, knowledge sharing, and participation in architecture review processes.
• Develop and maintain platform documentation, operational runbooks, and reusable infrastructure components.

## What You'll Bring
• Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Mathematics, or quantitative field.
• Extensive experience with 5+ years in distributed systems, cloud platforms, and data engineering, with at least 3 years focused on enterprise data platforms.
• Minimum 3+ years in technical leadership roles, demonstrating ability to guide engineering teams, influence technical decisions, and deliver complex systems.
• Deep proficiency in SQL, data modeling, stream processing, and hands-on experience with cloud platforms and containerization technologies.
• Production experience building and operating large-scale data platforms using infrastructure as code and automated deployment practices.
• Strong technical leadership skills with experience mentoring engineers and fostering collaborative, high-performance team environments.
• Excellent technical communication abilities, with experience presenting to technical and business audiences and building consensus around technical decisions.
