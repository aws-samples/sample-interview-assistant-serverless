## Question

Architect an e-commerce platform that accommodates substantial traffic volumes while maintaining operational resilience
Utilize a free diagramming tool, e.g. https://excalidraw.com/ to produce visual designs as needed.

## Instructions

Provide the candidate with a use case requiring e-commerce system architecture. Evaluate their capability to:

- Recognize potential structural weaknesses and performance constraints
- Plan for horizontal expansion and continuous availability
- Factor in protection mechanisms and data integrity
- Conceptualize traffic distribution and data partitioning approaches
- Eliminate individual failure risks

Allocate 20 minutes for this conversation. Prompt the candidate to pose clarification queries and work through the complete architectural solution.

## Evaluation Criteria

Assess the candidate based on:

- Detects traffic distribution challenges (regional routing, concentration issues)
- Acknowledges data partitioning complexities and distribution asymmetries
- Highlights individual failure vulnerabilities (traffic distributors, data stores, service endpoints)
- Explores horizontal growth challenges (dynamic resource allocation, static capacity constraints)
- Tackles protection gaps (public service interfaces, data layer entry points, traffic flood mitigation)
- Evaluates data uniformity challenges (synchronization delays, eventual convergence)
- References telemetry, audit trails, and system transparency needs
- Explores data preservation and catastrophe mitigation approaches

## Expected Answer

Comprehensive responses generally demonstrate:

- Comprehension of decentralized architectures and breakdown scenarios
- Familiarity with traffic allocation techniques beyond basic rotation methods
- Understanding of data segmentation compromises and uniform distribution algorithms
- Acknowledgment that spatial distribution independently doesn't guarantee continuous operation
- Protection strategies for external interfaces and backend components
- Speed versus uniformity compromises in decentralized data systems
- Appropriate boundary definition and service decomposition patterns
- Financial efficiency tactics (elastic resource management, capacity optimization)

## Time

20 minutes
